About Our Auto Sales

Used Car NJ by OGM was built around a simple idea: auto sales shouldn't hinge on a credit score. We're a buy here pay here dealership in Union, NJ, which means we handle the sale and the financing ourselves — no outside bank standing between you and a vehicle you need.

Our auto sales process starts with a lot of pre-owned vehicles chosen for reliability, not just price. From there, our in-house financing team looks at your full picture — income, stability, and what you can realistically manage — rather than reducing you to a single number.

That approach is why so many drivers come to us for bad credit auto loans, no credit check options, and second chance financing after being turned away elsewhere. Whether you're rebuilding credit or simply prefer working directly with the dealership on your terms, our auto sales team is here to find a vehicle and a payment plan that actually works for you.
